---
name: estimate-summary
description: How to write the stakeholder-facing summary of an effort estimate. Use when writing the final estimate summary text.
---

- Lead with the total (including risk buffer), then break down by side.
- State the risk buffer plainly as a hedge against unknowns, not as padding.
- Keep it to 3-5 sentences of plain prose — no headers, no bullet lists.
- Do not editorialize about whether the estimate is good or bad; report the numbers.
