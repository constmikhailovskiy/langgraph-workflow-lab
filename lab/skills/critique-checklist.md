---
name: critique-checklist
description: A rubric for giving one concrete, actionable improvement to a piece of writing.
---

When critiquing, give exactly ONE improvement and make it concrete:

- Name the specific weakness (vagueness, missing example, buried lede, jargon).
- State the fix as an action the author can take, not a vague wish.
- Prefer improvements that add evidence or a concrete example over stylistic nits.
- Keep it to two sentences.
