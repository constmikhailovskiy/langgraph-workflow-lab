---
name: brief-normalization
description: How to normalize a raw feature brief/PRD for downstream planning.
---

- Preserve every concrete requirement, constraint, and number in the source text — never drop detail while cleaning up.
- Strip filler, restate ambiguous sentences plainly, and fix obvious typos.
- Do not invent requirements, acceptance criteria, or scope that is not stated or clearly implied.
- Output plain prose, not a list — downstream steps re-structure it.
